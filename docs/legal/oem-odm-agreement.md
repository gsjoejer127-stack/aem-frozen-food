## 3. PRODUCTS, SPECIFICATION AND GOLDEN SAMPLE 产品、规格与标准样品

3.1 Production starts only after both parties sign the specification sheet and the Company's Golden Sample for that Product. The signed Golden Sample is the sole quality benchmark. Verbal descriptions, restaurant dishes, competitor products, photographs and marketing images are not benchmarks.

3.2 Natural variation in colour, viscosity, spice heat, aroma, piece count, sauce distribution, ice crystal formation and weight between raw material lots, batches and packing runs is normal and is not a defect. Agreed tolerances are stated in Annex A; where none is stated, ±10% on net weight per unit, ±10% on piece count and ±15% on sauce-to-solid ratio apply.
不同原料批次、生产批次及包装作业之间，在色泽、浓稠度、辣度、香气、颗数、酱汁分布、冰晶及重量上之自然差异属正常，不构成缺陷。允差以附件 A 为准；未载明者，单位净重允差 ±10%、颗数允差 ±10%、酱固比允差 ±15%。

3.3 The Company may substitute a raw material of equivalent function, and may adjust process parameters, equipment and ingredient suppliers, provided the Product remains within the Approved Specification. Chilli varieties, galangal, kaffir lime leaf, lemongrass, coconut milk solids, palm sugar and fish-sauce grade are subject to seasonal and origin variation.
本公司得替换功能相当之原料，并得调整工艺参数、设备及原料供应商，惟产品须维持于核准规格范围内。辣椒品种、南姜、柠檬叶、香茅、椰浆固形物、椰糖及鱼露等级均存在季节性与产地差异。

3.4 Any change requested by the Customer to recipe, weight, packaging format or process is a new specification. It requires re-development, re-quotation, a new Golden Sample and a new lead time, and the Customer bears the cost of trial runs, obsolete packaging and written-off materials.
客户对配方、重量、包装形式或工艺提出之任何变更，均属新规格，须重新开发、重新报价、重新确认标准样品并重新计算交期；试产费用、废弃包装及报废物料成本由客户承担。

3.5 The shelf life stated in Annex A is determined from the date of production and applies only where the Product is held continuously at −18°C or below. It is a specification parameter, not a warranty of condition after the Product leaves the Company's premises.
附件 A 所载保质期自生产日起算，且仅适用于产品持续保存于 −18°C 或以下之情形。该保质期为规格参数，非对产品离厂后状态之保证。
