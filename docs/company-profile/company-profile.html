地道中华风味
清真标准化速冻Turning authentic Chinese recipes into halal, standardised frozen products

AL-EKHLAS MANUFACTURING SDN. BHD.（品牌 AEM Frozen Food / 真心食品）是一家位于马来西亚雪兰莪州 Semenyih 的清真中华食品制造商。我们的产品涵盖烧烤串、饺子、中式料理包、火锅汤底与酱料、腌制肉片、小吃、养生汤包、传统糖水、花茶及调味料。

每一款产品均采用优质原料，结合传统中式烹饪工艺与现代速冻技术，在保留地道风味的同时，确保品质稳定、食品安全，并符合清真要求。

AL-EKHLAS MANUFACTURING SDN. BHD. (AEM Frozen Food / 真心食品) is a halal Chinese food manufacturer based in Semenyih, Selangor. Our range covers satay skewers, dumplings, ready-to-eat meals, hotpot soup bases and sauces, marinated meats, snacks, herbal soup packs, dessert soups, flower teas and seasonings — made with quality ingredients and modern flash-freezing to deliver authentic flavour with consistent quality.

“一个真正强大的餐饮品牌，不能只靠一位好厨师，而需要一套标准化、可复制的系统。”A truly strong F&B brand cannot rely on one great chef alone — it needs a standardised, replicable system.
